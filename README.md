## 🎮 3 Flexible Match Vibes

Choose your atmosphere when creating or hosting a room:

1. **🎉 Friends & Party Mode:**
   - Hilarious party challenges, friendly roasts, group confessions, and snack/boba bets (*"You owe the winner a coffee or pizza!"*).
   - Party reaction emojis: Pizza (`🍕`), Fire (`🔥`), Clown (`🤡`), Trophy (`🏆`), Laugh (`😂`), Sparkle (`✨`).
   - 4-Player Free-For-All or 2v2 Squads.

2. **💖 Couples & Date Night Mode:**
   - Sweet prompts, nostalgic memory shares, flirty vouchers (*"10-min massage voucher"*), and victory love letters.
   - Kiss & Hug reactions (`💋`, `💖`, `🌹`, `🪶`).

3. **☕ Chill & Casual Mode:**
   - Pure aesthetic board game night with friends or family, relaxing Lo-Fi background beats, and clean classic Ludo rules.

## ✨ Standout Features

### 🎲 1. Smooth 15x15 Aesthetic Board
- 4 rich aesthetic quadrants: **Rose Romance**, **Lavender Dream**, **Sunset Peach**, and **Mint Sparkle**.
- 3D-styled animated dice roller with realistic tumbling physics and ASMR sounds.
- Safe Star Sanctuaries and Special Dare Tiles (`💌`).
- 2v2 Team Shield / Bond mechanic: Teammate tokens on the same tile protect each other.

### 🌌 2. Ambient Lighting Themes
- **Celestial Night:** Deep indigo velvet sky with twinkling constellations and stardust trails.
- **Cozy Candlelight:** Warm amber hearth glow and procedural rain soundscapes.
- **Cherry Blossom Sunset:** Romantic pastel lavender gradients and drifting sakura petals.

### 🎧 3. Browser Voice Chat & Synchronized Lo-Fi Radio
- **WebRTC Voice Chat:** Low-latency voice directly in the browser with mute/unmute and visual waveforms.
- **Synchronized Ambient Soundscape:** Both players listen in unison to procedural gentle rain, cozy fireplace hearths, or cosmic starlight.

### 👤 4. 1-Click Frictionless Guest Profiles
- No mandatory signup. Choose from cute & cool gaming avatars (`😎`, `🍕`, `🎮`, `🚀`, `👑`, `🥰`, `🐱`, `🥑`) and generate fun nicknames.
- Instant shareable room codes and invite links (`http://localhost:3000?room=CODE`).
